# Roadmap

The active sequence is defined by `docs/specs/84_erpguard_evolution_master_spec.md`:

- **Phase 1:** project hygiene and version convergence (current).
- **Phase 2:** database migrations and bounded persistence.
- **Phase 3:** identity and tenant enforcement.
- Later phases: connector SDK, canonical events, process packages, replay, Proof of Improvement, permits and the controlled Odoo quotation vertical.

Only the active phase may be implemented. Real ERP writes, autonomous promotion and generic raw ERP capabilities remain outside the current scope.
